## The difference

dotenv-vault is a paid hosted service.

envden is self-hosted only, under the MIT license. The server runs on your own hardware and holds ciphertext only.

## What envden is

- Syncs whole env files, byte for byte, across your own machines.
- End-to-end encrypted, through a server you host.
- Reached over Tailscale.
- For one person. v1 has no team sharing.
