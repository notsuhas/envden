# audit-protocol

134 TS cases examined: 124 fully mapped; 10 contain language-specific assertions that cannot be replayed literally. All representable assertions have Rust coverage. No production Rust bug was found. No Windows-specific cases occur in this slice.

Fixtures were captured by instrumenting temporary copies of the frozen TS tests, leaving packages/ and apps/ untouched. The originals assertions ran against the original TS functions. Byte arrays are hex or repeated-byte data; integers remain exact. Each captured input/result/source lookup is snapshotted at call time. Nonprotocol secret-key extras on member records are removed because Rust records do not carry them. All valid codec encodings are compared byte-for-byte and every shorter prefix is rejected with INVALID_DATA. The TS known-answer suites use the shared vectors in place. OPAQUE registration is random, so its original static-public-key and record-shape assertions are preserved rather than comparing random output.

The 10 language-specific rows are not counted as fully mapped. They are not missing platform tests. Their representable portions remain tested.
